02About

A builder who wants to know how things work.

More about me

I'm Taiwo Adebola — a cybersecurity student and aspiring software engineer from Nigeria. My work moves between building applications, exploring how systems fail, and running the infrastructure software depends on.

I learn by building. That means a church service planner with a live document preview, a Telegram bot that remembers its people, a verification pipeline that reports where teams already work, and a self-hosted deployment environment I operate and troubleshoot myself.

I care about the difference between concept, in development and working — this portfolio labels all three honestly, because engineering credibility starts with accurate claims.
